Zerostar

A zerostar is a UUIDv7 minted with its timestamp set to zero, using nosedive mint --ms 0. Its first two groups are 00000000-0000. The remaining bits still distinguish documents; timestamp zero is a convention for foundational material, not a claim that it was written in 1970.

I use zerostar identities for documents describing the assumptions I build on. They can evolve, but keep their identities so references survive revisions. Git preserves the history of those revisions.

Zero-timestamp IDs sort before UUIDv7 IDs minted at ordinary present-day timestamps. Among zerostars, the remaining bits determine their order; they do not establish an order of authorship or importance.

These documents use KINGSMetaL for their frontmatter and Markdown for their prose.
